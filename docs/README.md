# Interactive Fig. 3 – Predicted Plant–Pollinator Interactions (Canary Islands)

**Live figure:** [ecological-complexity-lab.github.io/svd_based_spatial_prediction](https://ecological-complexity-lab.github.io/svd_based_spatial_prediction/)

## Why we made it

The static figure in the paper (Fig. 3a) is a dense 39 × 249 matrix that is difficult to read in print. The interactive version allows readers to:

- Identify which specific interactions are predicted and with what confidence.
- Explore the island-level distribution of any species and its local interaction degree.
- Understand the relationship between a species' generalism (overall degree) and how many novel interactions are predicted for it.
- Filter the matrix to focus on a specific prediction approach or observed links only.
- Adjust the probability threshold interactively to explore how the set of predicted links changes with stringency.

---

## What the figure shows

This is the interactive companion to Fig. 3

The main panel is a **plant × pollinator interaction matrix** for the Canary Islands, combining two types of information:

| Colour | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Green gradient | **Observed interaction** – colour intensity reflects the proportion of islands on which the interaction was recorded |
| Plum / purple | **Predicted, never-observed** – predicted using data from the same island only (*Single location*) |
| Salmon / orange | **Predicted, never-observed** – predicted using data from a different island (*External location*) |
| Steel blue | **Predicted, never-observed** – predicted by both approaches (*Both approaches*) |

Colour transparency encodes the predicted probability (threshold = 0.7; fully opaque = probability 1.0).

The two scatter plots on the right reproduce **Fig. 3c**, showing the correlation between a species' overall degree (number of unique interaction partners across all islands) and the number of never-observed yet predicted links assigned to it.

The map at the bottom shows the **seven sampling locations** across the Canary Islands and the coast of Western Sahara.

## The data

- **Interaction network:** Plant–pollinator visitation data from the Canary Islands, originally published by [Trøjelsgaard et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2015.0798). Networks were recorded at two sites per island across six islands (El Hierro, La Gomera, Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Fuerteventura) and one site on the Western Sahara coast – 14 sites pooled into 7 island-level networks.
- **Species:** 39 plant species × 249 pollinator species.
- **Predictions:** Generated by applying a thresholded softImpute SVD algorithm pairwise across all location combinations. Predicted probabilities were sigmoid-transformed and thresholded at 0.7. Two prediction modes were compared: *diagonal* (train and test on the same island) and *off-diagonal* (train on one island, predict on another).
- **Underlying data file:** `figure3a_data.json` — derived from `results/df_combined.rds`, which is the output of the full analysis pipeline in `code/Abramov_et_al_spatial_prediction_analysis.R`.

## How to use it

### Clicking in the matrix
| Target | What happens |
|--------|-------------|
| **Plant label** (left) | Shows species info, island presence, and local degree; highlights row; marks island dots on map |
| **Pollinator column header** (top) | Shows species info, island presence, and local degree; highlights column; marks island dots on map |
| **Matrix cell** | Shows interaction details and predicted probabilities; highlights row + column; marks both species on map |

### Clicking the scatter plots
- Clicking any **point in the scatter plots** (bottom-right) selects that species and triggers the same highlight as clicking the matrix label.

### Clicking the map
- Clicking an **island dot** highlights every matrix cell that represents an interaction either *observed on that island* or *predicted for species pairs that both occur on that island*.

### Filter panel
- The **Show / hide checkboxes** (between the matrix and the information panel) toggle each interaction type on and off independently.
- The **Threshold slider** (below the checkboxes) adjusts the minimum predicted probability required to display a link, from 0.5 to 1.0 (default 0.7). You can also type a value directly into the number field. The matrix colours and the correlation scatter plots update in real time to reflect the new threshold.
